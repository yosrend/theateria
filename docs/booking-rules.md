# Booking rules

## Tiers

| Tier | Rows | Price |
|---|---|---|
| VVIP | A and B | 120 GBP |
| VIP | C to E | 75 GBP |
| Regular | F to J | 40 GBP |

Each row has 10 seats, so the hall has 100 seats.

## Limits and fees

- Up to 8 seats per booking
- 2 GBP fee per seat, added to the total
- Promo code OPENING10 takes 10 percent off

## Hold timer

- Chosen seats are held for 5 minutes
- The hold can be extended once by 2 minutes
- When the timer ends, the seats are released and the selection is cleared

## Orphan seat rule

A selection is refused when it would leave a single empty seat in a row. The visitor sees a message that names the seat, for example "This leaves a single empty seat at B3", and the hold button stays disabled until the selection changes.

## Checkout steps

1. Review seats and total
2. Details and guest names, with access needs
3. Payment, simulated
4. Confirmation with a receipt and a ticket that has a QR code
