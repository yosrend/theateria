# Product

## Audience

People who want to see a live performance at one theatre and care where they sit. The site is built for a first visit on a phone or a laptop.

## Scope

- One venue: a Victorian theatre in London with one hall of 100 seats
- Six programs per month, each with its own date and ensemble
- Booking of exact seats, paid in GBP

## Out of scope

- Real payment. The payment step is simulated in the browser
- Accounts and order history
- Server side seat inventory. Seat state lives in the page
- More than one venue or hall

## Pages and sections

| Section | Job |
|---|---|
| Intro | 96 frame sequence, skippable, sets the tone |
| Seats | Pick a program, choose seats, see the total |
| Six programs | List of shows with a preview that follows the cursor |
| Rooms | The spaces of the building, scroll driven |
| Gallery | 12 photos, opens in a full screen viewer |
| Beyond the stage | Four events with their own images |
| FAQ | Answers on tickets, access and arrival |
