Before you book

Short answers about holds, waitlists and entry.

How long is my seat held?

Five minutes from the moment you press Hold. A timer counts down on screen. If it runs out, the seat is released and someone else can take it.

The show is sold out. What now?

Join the waitlist for that performance. When a seat frees up, the next person in line gets an offer by message and has ten minutes to accept it.

Why can't I leave one empty seat between two groups?

A single empty seat is almost impossible to sell. We ask you to move your choice by one seat so the row stays easy to fill.

How does entry work?

After payment you get a ticket with a QR code. Staff scan it at the door. Each code works once, so a screenshot shared twice will not get a second person in.

Can I get a refund or change the date?

Tickets can be moved to another performance of the same program up to 24 hours before the start. Refunds are issued when we cancel a show.

Are there wheelchair spaces?

Yes. Level spaces are at the ends of rows A, F and J, each with a seat beside it for a companion. Write to the box office and we reserve them for you.
